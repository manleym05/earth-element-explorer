Earth Element Explorer does not collect, store or sell personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

What stays on your device

The app remembers small display preferences (such as a map token you choose to enter) in local storage on your device only. Deleting the app removes them.

Third-party map services

To draw 3D terrain and satellite imagery, the app requests map tiles from Cesium ion (Cesium GS, Inc.) and Microsoft Bing Maps, which Cesium ion serves. Like any web request, these services receive your device's IP address and the map area being viewed. Their handling is covered by their own policies: cesium.com/legal/privacy and privacy.microsoft.com. All mineral, production and trade data ships inside the app.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
